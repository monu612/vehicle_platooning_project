## 2024-06-11 - [Optimize `max()` and `min()` in tightly-looped helpers]
**Learning:** In highly called helper functions used during pathfinding loops (like `_edge_metric` and `_clamp_pheromone`), the overhead of the built-in Python `max()` and `min()` functions is surprisingly significant compared to simple inline conditionally evaluation or explicit `if`/`else` branches. Profiling revealed that optimizing just these trivial mathematical operations yielded an overall latency improvement by lowering function call overhead within critical loops without overly reducing readability.
**Action:** Always favor inline ternary operators (e.g. `val if val > min_val else min_val`) or explicit `if/else` checks over standard built-ins for bounding numbers inside heavily traversed graph algorithms to eliminate function call overhead.
