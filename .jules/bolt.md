## 2026-08-06 - Precomputing Static Paths in Dynamic Graphs
**Learning:** For pathfinding on the dynamic networkx graph, the lazy initialization of `all_simple_paths` during dynamically disrupted states is an anti-pattern as it permanently omits paths temporarily broken by link failures. Precomputing paths on the static base topology *before* mutations occur and validating edges on-the-fly (`all(G.has_edge(u, v) for u, v in zip(path, path[1:]))`) avoids this and significantly reduces overhead (from ~0.78s to ~0.08s for 3000 calls).
**Action:** When working with dynamic topologies in NetworkX where link failures are common, always precompute simple paths or node topology configurations on the baseline graph upfront and apply real-time filtering rather than regenerating paths on every iteration.
